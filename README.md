The Chiikawa Mod **brings the world of [Chiikawa](https://chiikawa-info.jp/) into Minecraft**: the characters of Chiikawa come to live in your world, so you can experience the world of Chiikawa in Minecraft.

> [!TIP]
> Come and chat on [Discord](https://discord.gg/wPkgfst5Z) or in the QQ group **995178570**. More on the [website](https://chiikawa.dwinovo.cn/en/).

## Installation

The Chiikawa Mod is on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/chiikawa) and [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/pT971QUb): search for **"Chiikawa"** in your launcher. These versions and loaders are supported:

| Minecraft | Loaders | Java |
|---|---|---|
| 1.20.1, 1.20.2, 1.20.4 | Fabric, Forge | 17 |
| 1.20.6, 1.21.1, 1.21.4, 1.21.5, 1.21.6, 1.21.7, 1.21.8, 1.21.10, 1.21.11 | Fabric, NeoForge | 21 |
| 26.1.2, 26.2 | Fabric, NeoForge | 25 |

The mod runs on both sides: on a server, **install it on the server and on every client**.

## Getting Started

1. **Explore.** Wander around your Minecraft world and you will meet the characters of Chiikawa.
2. **Try taming them.** Some of them can be tamed with food and become your pets, *keeping you company all the way*.
3. **Tell them what to do.** Pets can act in several ways: follow you, stay where they are, or roam freely near home and live their own lives.

Pets take on all kinds of jobs: **hand one a different tool and it does different work**, such as farming, hunting or playing music.

Your pets also live the way they do in Chiikawa: they go to work for money, go shopping, sit exams for licences, and bring you presents.

The first time you join a world you get *Chiikawa's Work Handbook*; right-click it to read.

More on the [website](https://chiikawa.dwinovo.cn/en/).
